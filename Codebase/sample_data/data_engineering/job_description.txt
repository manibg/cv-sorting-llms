Data Engineer (4+ Years)

Location: Hybrid / Remote-friendly
Experience required: 4+ years in data engineering or closely related roles

About the role
We are hiring a Data Engineer to design, build, and operate reliable data pipelines that power analytics, machine learning features, and operational reporting. You will own ingestion-to-warehouse workflows, improve data quality, and partner with analytics and platform teams.

Must-have skills
- 4+ years of professional experience in data engineering, ETL/ELT, or large-scale data platforms
- Strong Python for data pipelines and automation
- SQL (advanced): window functions, query tuning, data modeling
- Cloud data stack experience in at least one of: AWS, GCP, or Azure
- Batch and/or streaming pipelines (Airflow / Composer / Prefect / Dagster, and/or Spark / Kafka)
- Data warehouse experience (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, or Databricks)
- Data modeling fundamentals (dimensional modeling, slowly changing dimensions)
- Git, CI/CD basics, and production operational practices (monitoring, alerting, SLAs)

Nice-to-have skills
- dbt for transformation layer
- Kafka / Kinesis / PubSub for near real-time ingestion
- Spark (PySpark) for large-scale transforms
- Infrastructure as code (Terraform)
- Data quality frameworks (Great Expectations, Deequ, or similar)
- Experience with lakehouse patterns (Delta / Iceberg)
- Docker and basic Kubernetes exposure

Responsibilities
- Design and maintain scalable ETL/ELT pipelines from operational systems to the warehouse/lakehouse
- Build reliable ingestion for structured and semi-structured data (APIs, databases, event streams, files)
- Model analytics-ready tables and improve freshness, quality, and cost efficiency
- Implement data quality checks, lineage awareness, and incident response for pipeline failures
- Collaborate with analysts, ML engineers, and product teams on data contracts and requirements
- Document pipelines, ownership, and runbooks; participate in on-call for critical data products

Minimum qualifications
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience
- 4+ years delivering production data pipelines in a business environment
- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical partners

What success looks like in 6 months
- Owned pipelines meet defined SLAs with measurable quality checks
- At least one major domain (e.g., orders, inventory, or customer) is modernized for reliability and cost
- Stakeholders trust warehouse tables as the source of truth for reporting and ML features
