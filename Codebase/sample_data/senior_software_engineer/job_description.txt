Senior Software Engineer

Location: Hybrid / Remote-friendly
Experience required: 5+ years of professional software development

About the role
We are hiring a Senior Software Engineer to design, build, and operate high-quality backend services that power customer-facing products. You will lead technical design for medium-to-large features, raise engineering standards, and mentor other engineers while shipping reliable systems in production.

Must-have skills
- 5+ years of professional experience building production software
- Strong proficiency in at least one of: Java, Python, Go, or TypeScript/Node.js
- Solid experience designing and operating REST (and/or gRPC) microservices
- Relational databases and SQL (PostgreSQL/MySQL) including schema design and query performance
- Cloud experience in AWS, GCP, or Azure
- Containerization and deployment basics (Docker; Kubernetes or equivalent orchestration is a plus)
- Automated testing (unit/integration) and CI/CD
- System design skills: scalability, reliability, observability, and trade-off analysis
- Proven ability to mentor engineers and lead design discussions

Nice-to-have skills
- Event-driven architecture (Kafka, SQS/SNS, Pub/Sub)
- Caching (Redis) and search (Elasticsearch/OpenSearch)
- Infrastructure as code (Terraform)
- Frontend familiarity (React/TypeScript) for full-stack collaboration
- Experience with high-traffic consumer or e-commerce platforms
- Security best practices (authn/authz, secrets, OWASP basics)

Responsibilities
- Own end-to-end delivery of features across design, implementation, testing, rollout, and monitoring
- Write clean, maintainable, well-tested code and conduct high-quality code reviews
- Lead technical design for complex projects; document ADRs and interface contracts
- Improve service reliability (SLOs, alerting, incident response, postmortems)
- Partner with product, design, and data teams to refine requirements and sequencing
- Mentor junior/mid engineers through pairing, reviews, and technical guidance
- Continuously reduce toil via tooling, automation, and platform improvements

Minimum qualifications
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
- 5+ years shipping and operating production services
- Excellent communication skills with engineers and cross-functional partners
- Comfortable owning ambiguous problems and driving them to measurable outcomes

What success looks like in 6 months
- Delivered at least one cross-team initiative with clear reliability and product impact
- Measurable improvement in service health (latency, error rate, or deploy confidence)
- Recognized as a go-to technical mentor within the squad
